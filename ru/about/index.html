About LLM Pricing

LLM Pricing is a free, independent tool for comparing the price, capabilities and quality of large language models across every provider that hosts them. The goal is simple: help developers and teams pick the right model at the right price without digging through a dozen separate pricing pages.

What you can do here

Compare per-token input, output and cache pricing across providers; see the same model priced across every host to find the lowest channel; rank models by intelligence, coding ability, value and speed; and estimate real monthly cost with the built-in calculators.

Where the data comes from

Pricing and capability data is aggregated from models.dev (MIT). Quality and speed benchmarks come from Artificial Analysis, and real-world usage popularity is drawn from OpenRouter. We merge the same model across hosts, compute a blended price and normalise capabilities so the figures are comparable.

Independence

We are not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any of the model providers listed. Prices and specifications change often and are shown for reference only: always confirm on each provider's official page before relying on them.
