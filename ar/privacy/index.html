Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

LLM Pricing ("we", "us") operates the website at llmpricing.dev. This page explains what information is processed when you visit and how it is used. LLM Pricing is a free, static informational site: there are no user accounts, and you don't need to sign in or submit any personal details to use it.

Information we collect

We do not ask for or store personal information such as your name, email address or payment details, and there is no server-side database of visitors. Preferences you set, like light or dark theme and language, are stored locally in your own browser and are never sent to us.

Cookies and advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can decline personalised advertising at any time.

To opt out of personalised advertising, visit Google Ads Settings and aboutads.info.

Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before any non-essential or advertising cookies are set.

We use a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) to show a message where you can agree, decline, or manage your choices in detail. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads, and you can reopen the consent tool at any time to review or change your selection.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate traffic, such as page views and rough geography. This data is processed by Google to help us improve the site and is not used to identify individual visitors.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Information processed by the third-party services above is governed by their own privacy policies, including the Google Privacy Policy.

External links

Pages here link to provider and data-source websites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those external sites.

Children

This site is intended for a general, professional audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can disable or delete cookies in your browser settings and opt out of personalised ads using the links above. Blocking cookies will not prevent you from using the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any material change will be reflected by the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
