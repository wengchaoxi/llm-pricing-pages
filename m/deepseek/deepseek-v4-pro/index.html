DeepSeek V4 Pro is offered by 77 providers on this page. Public prices are shown for 70 of them. No official API price is listed for it; the prices below come from third-party channels. The lowest paid channel is routing.run at $0.348 / $0.696 per 1M. That channel is a third-party gateway, so confirm its availability and rate limits before depending on it. Prices vary about 6.9× across the providers that host it. It also has 1 free ($0) channel and 4 covered by a paid subscription; free tiers usually carry rate limits, and subscription-covered access bills $0 per token only after the subscription fee.

The context window is 1,000,000 tokens at the reference host, but hosts report different limits, from 128,000 to 1,050,000, so the usable window depends on the provider you pick. It supports reasoning, tool use, and structured output. The weights are open, so it can also be self-hosted or served through a gateway of your choice. Providers report the capability flags inconsistently, so verify a specific feature against the host you plan to use. Its training knowledge cuts off at 2025-05.
