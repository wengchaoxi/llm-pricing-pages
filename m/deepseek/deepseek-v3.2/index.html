DeepSeek V3.2 is offered by 43 providers on this page. Public prices are shown for 41 of them. No official API price is listed for it; the prices below come from third-party channels. The lowest paid channel is NanoGPT at $0.209 / $0.31 per 1M. That channel is a third-party gateway, so confirm its availability and rate limits before depending on it. Prices vary about 14× across the providers that host it. It also has 2 covered by a paid subscription; free tiers usually carry rate limits, and subscription-covered access bills $0 per token only after the subscription fee.

Artificial Analysis rates it 21.5 on the Intelligence Index, with 44.2 for coding. Against its lowest blended price of $0.234 per 1M, that is roughly 92 index points per dollar, which is the value ratio the leaderboards rank on.

The context window is 32,768 tokens at the reference host, but hosts report different limits, from 32,768 to 164,000, so the usable window depends on the provider you pick. It supports reasoning, tool use, and structured output. Accepted input modalities are Text, PDF, and Image. The weights are open, so it can also be self-hosted or served through a gateway of your choice. Providers report the capability flags inconsistently, so verify a specific feature against the host you plan to use. Its training knowledge cuts off at 2024-07.
