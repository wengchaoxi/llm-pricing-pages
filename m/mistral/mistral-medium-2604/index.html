Mistral Medium 3.5 is offered by 11 providers on this page. Public prices are shown for 9 of them. Its official list price is $1.50 per 1M input tokens and $7.50 per 1M output tokens. No paid channel is priced below the official list price. Prices vary about 1.4× across the providers that host it. It also has 1 free ($0) channel; free tiers usually carry rate limits, and subscription-covered access bills $0 per token only after the subscription fee.

The context window is 262,144 tokens at the reference host, but hosts report different limits, from 256,000 to 262,144, so the usable window depends on the provider you pick. It supports reasoning, tool use, and structured output. Accepted input modalities are Text, Image, and PDF. The weights are open, so it can also be self-hosted or served through a gateway of your choice.
