Devstral 2 by mistral is offered by 15 providers on this page. Public prices are shown for 13 of them. Its official list price is $0.40 per 1M input tokens and $2.00 per 1M output tokens. The lowest paid channel is NanoGPT at $0.40 / $1.40 per 1M, about 1.2× below the list price. That channel is a third-party gateway, so confirm its availability and rate limits before depending on it. It also has 1 free ($0) channel; free tiers usually carry rate limits, and subscription-covered access bills $0 per token only after the subscription fee.

Artificial Analysis rates it 8.6 on the Intelligence Index, with 31.3 for coding and 2.3 for agentic tasks. Against its lowest blended price of $0.65 per 1M, that is roughly 13 index points per dollar, which is the value ratio the leaderboards rank on.

The context window is 262,144 tokens at the reference host, but hosts report different limits, from 128,000 to 262,144, so the usable window depends on the provider you pick. It supports tool use and structured output. Accepted input modalities are Text and PDF. The weights are open, so it can also be self-hosted or served through a gateway of your choice. Providers report the capability flags inconsistently, so verify a specific feature against the host you plan to use. Its training knowledge cuts off at 2025-12.
