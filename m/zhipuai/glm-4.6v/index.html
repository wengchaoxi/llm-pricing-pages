GLM-4.6V by zhipuai is offered by 18 providers on this page. Public prices are shown for 16 of them. Its official list price is $0.30 per 1M input tokens and $0.90 per 1M output tokens. The lowest paid channel is AIHubMix at $0.137 / $0.411 per 1M, about 2.2× below the list price. That channel is a third-party gateway, so confirm its availability and rate limits before depending on it. It also has 1 covered by a paid subscription; free tiers usually carry rate limits, and subscription-covered access bills $0 per token only after the subscription fee.

Artificial Analysis rates it 11.2 on the Intelligence Index. Against its lowest blended price of $0.205 per 1M, that is roughly 55 index points per dollar, which is the value ratio the leaderboards rank on. Median output speed is 89 tokens per second, with 3.63s to the first token.

The context window is 128,000 tokens at the reference host, but hosts report different limits, from 128,000 to 200,000, so the usable window depends on the provider you pick. It supports reasoning and tool use. Accepted input modalities are Text, Image, Video, and PDF. The weights are open, so it can also be self-hosted or served through a gateway of your choice. Providers report the capability flags inconsistently, so verify a specific feature against the host you plan to use. Its training knowledge cuts off at 2025-04.
