Claude Duet vs Grok Bot: what each costs and how the limits work

Which plans include each agent, who can use it today, how the usage limits work, and what the plan fee buys at API list prices.

Claude Duet (Anthropic, research preview since Oct 21, 2026) and Grok Bot (xAI, early beta since Aug 2026) run on their own cloud computer and keep working after you close the app. Both ask for approval before they act. Neither is sold as a separate product. Each is included in a consumer subscription, so the price is the price of the plan that unlocks it.

Plan prices come from the subscription catalog on this site and model rates from the pricing catalog. Each fact links to the vendor page it was read from.

Short answer

  • Claude Duet has no price of its own. It is included in Claude Pro (20,00 $ a month) and Claude Max (100,00 $ or 200,00 $), by waitlist and for US accounts first.
  • Grok Bot is included in Grok Premium (30,00 $ a month), Super (100,00 $) and Ultra (200,00 $), and in eligible Teams and Enterprise plans. Super and Ultra raise the weekly Grok Bot allowance to 3.5× and 12× the Premium level.
  • Neither vendor publishes an absolute usage cap. Claude Duet draws on the same limits as the rest of Claude, and Grok Bot's allowance is stated only as multiples of a base tier.
  • Lowest entry price: Claude Pro at 20,00 $ a month for Claude Duet, Grok Premium at 30,00 $ a month for Grok Bot.

Claude Duet: price and usage limits

Anthropic describes Claude Duet as a proactive agent for professionals and small business owners. It sends a short brief each morning, checks connected accounts every few hours, and works on assigned tasks in a virtual machine, so your phone or laptop does not need to stay on. During the research preview it is included in Pro and Max at no extra charge and uses the same usage limits as the rest of Claude.

Anthropic says the background checks count toward the plan's limits, which is why Claude Duet checks every few hours instead of continuously. Larger jobs, such as a financial model or a slide deck, take more model turns and use more of the allowance than a typical conversation. No absolute number of Claude Duet tasks or tokens is published, so the practical limit depends on how much background work you assign.

Access is by waitlist and starts with Pro and Max users in the US. The announcement covers those accounts only and gives no date for other plans or regions.

Claude Duet is designed around Claude Opus 5.5. Anthropic's API lists Opus 5.5 at 4,00 $ input and 20,00 $ output per 1M tokens, with cache reads at 0,20 $. That is below Claude Opus 5 at 5,00 $ and 25,00 $.

Grok Bot: price and usage limits

xAI describes Grok Bot as AI teammates that sign in to your tools, use them the way you would and return finished work. Bots run on a cloud computer that all Bots on the account share, several Bots can work in parallel, and a workflow you demonstrate once can be saved as a routine that runs on a schedule.

Why background work changes the math

A chat plan is used when you type. These agents also use it when you do not. Claude Duet checks connected accounts every few hours, and Anthropic says checking continuously would use up plan limits much faster. Grok Bot runs routines on a schedule and can run several Bots at once, which xAI describes as working 24/7.

How much of an allowance is used depends on how many connected apps or scheduled routines are active and how large each task is. Anthropic names financial models and decks as heavier jobs. xAI's tiers differ mainly in the weekly Grok Bot allowance, so the number of Bots you keep busy is the practical reason to move up.

Before paying for a higher tier, check what the agent does in the background. List the active routines and connected accounts. Claude Duet lets you set what it reports and how often.

Which one fits

  • You are on Claude Pro or Max in the US and want an assistant tied to your Claude memory, skills and connectors: Claude Duet adds no charge during the preview and runs inside your current limits.
  • You want several agents working in parallel, routines taught by demonstration, or a desktop app: Grok Bot, from 30,00 $ a month.
  • You are comparing price alone: Claude Pro at 20,00 $ is the lower entry point, but Claude Duet is waitlisted and US-first. Grok Premium at 30,00 $ includes Grok Bot, and xAI's page offers it for download with the plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is Claude Duet free?

No separate fee is listed. During the research preview, Claude Duet is included in Claude Pro (20,00 $ a month) and Claude Max (100,00 $ or 200,00 $), uses the same usage limits as the rest of Claude, and is available by waitlist to US users first.

How much does Grok Bot cost?

Grok Bot is included in Grok Premium at 30,00 $ a month, Super at 100,00 $ and Ultra at 200,00 $. On xAI's Grok Bot page, Super and Ultra raise the weekly usage to 3.5× and 12× the Premium level. Eligible Teams and Enterprise plans include it as well.

Which Claude plans include Claude Duet?

Anthropic's announcement names Claude Pro and Claude Max. It does not mention the Free, Team or Enterprise plans.

Does Claude Duet use my Claude usage limits?

Yes. It shares the plan's limits with the rest of Claude. Its background checks count toward them, and larger tasks such as financial models or decks use more than a typical conversation.

Which model does Claude Duet use?

Anthropic says Claude Duet is designed around Claude Opus 5.5, which Anthropic's API lists at 4,00 $ input and 20,00 $ output per 1M tokens.

Can Claude Duet send messages or make payments on its own?

No. Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows the exact action and waits for approval. Trading securities and moving money between accounts are blocked even with approval.

Is Claude Duet available outside the US?

The research preview starts with Pro and Max users in the US, by waitlist. The announcement does not give dates for other regions.

Does Grok Bot keep working when my computer is off?

xAI describes Bots as running on their own cloud computer and working 24/7, so your laptop does not need to stay on.
